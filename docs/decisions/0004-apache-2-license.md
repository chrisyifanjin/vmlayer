# 0004. Apache-2.0

Status: accepted, 2026-11-14

## Context

The project's goal is adoption inside other teams' platforms, including
commercial ones.

## Evidence

Every project this one wants to be compared with is Apache-2.0: E2B's runtime,
microsandbox, smolvm, Zeroboot, OpenSandbox, Gondolin. The one AGPL entry
surveyed (OpenDsec) had 11 stars and offers a commercial license, which
signals the AGPL is a barrier for its intended users.

## Decision

Apache-2.0, including the patent grant.

## Consequences

No copyleft leverage for a future commercial offering; any business built on
this would have to be services or a hosted control plane, not licensing.
